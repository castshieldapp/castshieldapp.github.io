Common questions

My TV doesn't show up. Make sure your iPhone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network, and that CastShield is allowed under iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network. Then tap Scan for TVs in Devices.

Which TVs work? Chromecast and Google TV, Roku, LG webOS TVs, DLNA smart TVs and Apple TV through AirPlay. Fire TV, PlayStation, Xbox and most other TVs can connect through their web browser from Devices › Fire TV, PlayStation, Xbox & TV browsers.

The video found isn't the one I wanted. Start playing the video on the page first, then tap Cast. If there are several, CastShield lists them so you can pick.

Subtitles are off or out of sync. Subtitles start off. Turn them on from the player or the cast remote, choose a language, and use the timing control to line them up.

Casting stops when I lock my phone. Keep CastShield open in the background while casting (don't swipe it away). The Lock Screen and Live Activity controls keep working while it runs.

A site doesn't work right. Some sites break with ad blocking. Turn blocking off for that site from the shield button in the address bar.
