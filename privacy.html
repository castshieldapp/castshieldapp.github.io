CastShield Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

CastShield does not collect, store or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Your browsing history, bookmarks, tabs, Continue Watching positions, queue, playlists and settings are stored only on your device. Private tabs are not saved at all. You can erase everything from Settings at any time, and deleting the app removes it all.

Problem log

CastShield keeps a short log of errors on your device to help fix playback problems. Web addresses in the log are shortened, and private tabs are never logged. The log is sent only if you choose Share Log in Settings › Report a Problem, and only to the person or app you pick.

Your network

CastShield uses your local network to find and control TVs and streaming devices on your Wi-Fi. Video goes from the website to your TV, sometimes through your iPhone. None of it passes through any server run by CastShield.

Services you connect to

Web pages you open load directly from those websites, under their own privacy policies. CastShield downloads public ad-blocking filter lists. If you add your own OpenSubtitles API key, your subtitle searches go directly to OpenSubtitles.com, and your key is stored in the iPhone Keychain.

Face ID

If you turn on Face ID lock, iOS handles the check. CastShield never sees your face data.

Children

CastShield is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: castshieldapp@gmail.com
